## Why the dates matter

- Entry to discharge is under seven months and the PD diagnosis came within
  roughly three months of arriving at the first real unit. That is the
  pattern the 2008 GAO review and the 2008 DoD policy change on
  personality-disorder separations were about.
- Presumption of soundness covers the whole period: anything not noted on
  the 2003 entrance exam is presumed to have started in service.
- Effective date of any award = date VA receives the Intent to File (if the
  claim follows within a year). Every day before the ITF is money lost.
