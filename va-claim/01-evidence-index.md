## C. Records-request details

- Army records for a 2004 separation are digital (iPERMS). Fastest route:
  **milConnect → Correspondence/Documentation → Defense Personnel Records
  Information (DPRIS)** → request full OMPF. Requires DS Logon / ID.me
  (the same login as VA.gov).
- Alternative: **vetrecs.archives.gov** (eVetRecs). State the purpose as
  "VA disability claim"; request "complete OMPF including separation
  packet" and "complete service treatment/medical records". Sign and
  upload the signature page the same day — unsigned requests are dropped.
- If a written SF-180 is used: NPRC, 1 Archives Drive, St. Louis MO 63138;
  fax 314-801-9195. Section II purpose: "VA compensation claim". Keep a copy.
- Turnaround is typically weeks to a few months; the Intent to File covers
  the wait. Log the request date in `tracker.csv`.
- VA health records: VA.gov → My HealtheVet → Medical records → Blue Button
  → "VA Health Summary" + all notes. Save the PDF to Drive.

## D. What the 5-13 packet will decide

Read it for these five things the moment it arrives:

1. **Who made the diagnosis and their credential.** In 2004, AR 635-200
   para 5-13 required a psychiatrist or a doctoral-level clinical
   psychologist. A diagnosis by anyone else undercuts the PD label.
2. **What symptoms were actually recorded** — depressed mood, anxiety,
   sleep, suicidal ideation, panic, adjustment problems. Those symptoms are
   the in-service evidence of an acquired disorder regardless of the label.
3. **Whether "long-standing" / "pre-existing" was asserted and on what
   basis.** An 18-year-old, seven months in, with a clean MEPS exam: the
   presumption of soundness applies and VA must rebut it by clear and
   unmistakable evidence.
4. **Whether any Axis I diagnosis (adjustment disorder, depressive
   disorder, anxiety disorder) appears alongside the PD.** That is a
   superimposed acquired disorder under 38 CFR 4.127 — directly claimable.
5. **The counseling statements' description of behavior** — what was
   happening in the unit (isolation, conflict, sleep, performance). Useful
   for the personal statement and for the ABCMR track.
