# Forms and filing — in order

Every form below is free. Nothing is submitted by an agent; the owner (or
the VSO on the owner's signed authority) submits. Status lives in
`tracker.csv`.

## 0. Before anything: VA.gov login works

ID.me or Login.gov linked to VA.gov (My HealtheVet emails in 2025 confirm an
account exists). Confirm contact info on the profile (VA sent a "confirm
your contact information" email 2025-11-18).

## 1. VA Form 21-0966 — Intent to File   ← DO THIS FIRST, TODAY

- What it does: sets the **effective date** for any award. The claim itself
  can follow any time within **12 months**; back pay runs from the ITF date.
- How: VA.gov → Disability → "File for disability compensation" → the
  online 526EZ flow records an ITF automatically the moment you start it
  and save; OR submit 21-0966 directly; OR call 800-827-1000 and state
  intent to file for compensation.
- Record the ITF date and its expiry (ITF + 1 year) in `tracker.csv` and
  `registers/calendar.csv`.

## 2. Records requests (no VA form)

- OMPF + STRs via milConnect DPRIS, or vetrecs.archives.gov, or SF-180.
  Details in `01-evidence-index.md` §C. Ask for everything; the separation
  packet and the entrance exam are the targets.
- VA health records: My HealtheVet Blue Button download.
- Private records: VA Form **21-4142** (authorization) + **21-4142a**
  (provider list) let VA pull them; faster to get copies yourself and
  upload.

## 3. VA Form 21-22 — Appointment of Veterans Service Organization

- Free representation. Options: DAV, VFW, American Legion, the state
  department of veterans affairs (Illinois IDVA / Kentucky KDVA / Michigan
  MVAA — whichever state is home now), or a county veterans service officer.
- The VSO gets VBMS access to the claim file and can see STRs once VA has
  them. Sign 21-22 early so the VSO can review the evidence before filing.
- Verify accreditation: va.gov/ogc/apps/accreditation.

## 4. Establish the current diagnosis

- VA route (recommended first): VA.gov → schedule with primary care →
  request mental-health referral, or call the VA medical center's
  mental-health clinic directly; enrollment (10-10EZ) if not already
  enrolled. VA treatment notes flow into the claim automatically.
- Private route: a licensed psychologist or psychiatrist. Ask them to
  complete the **Mental Disorders DBQ** (public DBQ, va.gov) and a nexus
  letter (`05-statements/nexus-letter-request.md`).
- Do both if possible: VA notes for the diagnosis and history; a private
  DBQ + nexus letter for the opinion.

## 5. Statements

- **21-4138** Statement in Support of Claim — the personal statement
  (`05-statements/personal-statement-draft.md`).
- **21-10210** Lay/Witness Statement — one per witness
  (`05-statements/buddy-statement-template.md`).
- **21-0781** Statement in Support of Claim for PTSD — ONLY if PTSD is
  the diagnosis or if there was a specific traumatic event (including
  assault / military sexual trauma). Otherwise skip.

## 6. VA Form 21-526EZ — the claim

- File as a **Fully Developed Claim**: everything attached, nothing pending.
- Conditions, as worded on the form:
  1. "Acquired psychiatric disorder, to include [diagnosis], onset in
     service 2004 (separated under AR 635-200 5-13, mischaracterized as
     personality disorder)".
  2. "Tinnitus" and/or "Bilateral hearing loss" — only if applicable.
  3. Anything else supported by the STRs.
- Attach: long-copy DD214, separation packet, STR extracts (entrance exam,
  any behavioral-health notes), current treatment records, DBQ, nexus
  letter, 21-4138, all 21-10210s, 21-4142/4142a if VA must pull anything.
- Where: VA.gov online (preferred; instant receipt), or via the VSO.
- Owner target: 2026-09-30. If the OMPF has not arrived, the VSO decides
  whether to file on the current evidence and let VA pull the records, or
  to wait — the ITF protects the date either way; **do not let the ITF
  lapse**.

## 7. After filing

- Attend the C&P exam (VA schedules it; missing it is the most common
  reason for denial). Prep: `05-statements/cp-exam-prep.md`.
- Check claim status on VA.gov; respond to any development letter within
  its deadline (usually 30 days).
- Rating decision arrives by mail and on VA.gov. If service-connected at
  any percentage: download the rating decision + benefit summary letter.

## 8. If denied or under-rated (one year from the decision date)

- **Supplemental Claim (20-0995)** with new and relevant evidence, or
- **Higher-Level Review (20-0996)**, or
- **Board appeal (10182)**.
The VSO chooses the lane. Do not miss the one-year window.

## 9. Downstream (owner's stated goal)

- **SBA VetCert SDVOSB**: after the rating decision shows service
  connection at any percentage, upload the decision/benefit letter and the
  DD214 at veterans.certify.sba.gov for the entity that will bid
  (Strandworks Medical Resources LLC per the calendar register; must be
  51%+ owned and controlled by the service-disabled veteran).
- VOSB does not require a rating — the calendar already says apply now.
- State benefits (property-tax relief, license fees) vary by state and by
  rating; check after the decision.
