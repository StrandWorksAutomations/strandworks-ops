# Questions only the owner can answer

The case cannot be filed without these. Answer in this file (edit in place)
or dictate; an agent will fold the answers into the drafts and tracker.

## A. The 2004 separation (gaps the OMPF may or may not fill)

1. What were you told the diagnosis was, by whom, and where (Fort Stewart
   behavioral health? Winn Army Community Hospital?)? Was it a psychiatrist,
   a psychologist, or someone else?
2. What actually led to the evaluation — did you seek help, or did the
   command send you? What was going on in the unit?
3. Was there any specific traumatic event, assault, or sexual trauma?
   (If yes: this changes the forms and framing; tell the VSO first.)
4. Did you have any injuries in OSUT or at Fort Stewart that went to sick
   call (knees, back, ankles, hearing)?
5. Did you keep any paperwork from the discharge — counseling forms,
   the notification memo, the mental-status evaluation? Any letters or
   emails home from that period?

## B. Before service

6. Any counseling, medication, psychiatric contact, or notable history
   before December 2003? Anything disclosed at MEPS (DD 2807-1)? Any
   enlistment waiver?

## C. Since 2004

7. Any mental-health diagnosis or treatment at any point since: therapist,
   psychiatrist, PCP prescribing an antidepressant/anxiolytic/sleep med,
   EAP, ER visit, inpatient? List provider, city, approximate dates.
8. Was the March 2025 CommonSpirit leave of absence medical, and if so
   for what?
9. Are you currently enrolled in VA health care, and at which VA medical
   center? Have you ever been seen by VA mental health?
10. Current symptoms, in your own words — sleep, mood, anxiety/panic,
    irritability, concentration, isolation, hypervigilance, alcohol use,
    any thoughts of self-harm and when. Frequency for each.
11. Effect on work and relationships — concrete examples with dates.

## D. Hearing

12. Do you have ringing in the ears (tinnitus)? Since when? Any hearing
    difficulty? Any hearing test since 2004 (employer, EMS physical)?
    Post-service noise exposure (sirens, helicopters, chainsaws on fire
    assignments) will be asked about; answer honestly, exposure in service
    is still conceded for 19K.

## E. Logistics

13. **State of legal residence today** (Illinois? Kentucky? Michigan?) —
    determines the state VSO and the VA regional office.
14. Do you already have a VSO or any prior VA claim/decision at all
    (including a denied one)? The 2023/2024 letters say no benefits, but
    say if anything was ever filed.
15. Who will write buddy statements: mother; one pre-service friend; one
    recent colleague. Names and whether they are willing.
16. VA.gov login working (ID.me / Login.gov)?

## F. Decisions

17. Proceed with the ITF today? (Recommended: yes, regardless of any
    other answer; it costs nothing and cannot hurt.)
18. VA mental-health intake vs. private evaluation first? (Recommended:
    VA intake now for the diagnosis and records; private DBQ + nexus letter
    if the VA clinician will not write an opinion — many will not.)
19. Pursue the ABCMR record correction in parallel? (Optional.)
