# Optional parallel track — correcting the narrative reason (ABCMR, DD Form 149)

Not required for the VA claim. Character of service is already Honorable.
This track changes what the DD214 says on block 28 / the SPD and RE codes,
which matters for employment records, for self-respect, and marginally for
the VA claim (an Army finding that the 2004 diagnosis was unsupported is
persuasive, not binding, on VA).

## Basis

- Since the Hagel (2014), Carson (2016), Kurta (2017) and Wilkie (2018)
  memoranda, discharge review and correction boards apply **liberal
  consideration** to applications involving mental-health conditions, and
  the three-year filing limit is routinely waived in the interest of justice.
- Personality-disorder separations from 2001–2007 were the subject of a
  2008 GAO review and a 2008 DoD policy tightening. Boards have granted
  changes of the narrative reason from "Personality Disorder" to
  "Secretarial Authority" (SPD JFF) and the RE code to RE-1 where the
  diagnosis did not meet the regulatory requirements or a later diagnosis
  shows the condition was something else.

## What to file

- **DD Form 149** to the Army Board for Correction of Military Records
  (arba.army.pentagon.mil). Request: change the narrative reason to
  "Secretarial Authority", SPD to JFF, RE code to RE-1.
- Attach: long-copy DD214, the separation packet, the current diagnosis
  and nexus letter (the same evidence as the VA claim), the personal
  statement, buddy statements.
- Argument: the 5-13 diagnosis did not meet AR 635-200 / DSM-IV
  requirements (age, duration, credential of the diagnosing provider as
  shown in the packet); the veteran has since been diagnosed with [X]; under
  liberal consideration the record should reflect that the separation was
  for a condition that was not a personality disorder.

## Sequencing

File AFTER the VA claim is filed and the separation packet has been read;
do not delay the VA claim for this. Typical ABCMR processing is many
months. Track in `tracker.csv` row P1.
