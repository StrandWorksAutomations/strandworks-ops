# Lay / witness statement template (VA Form 21-10210)

Purpose: people who knew Jonathan before service, right after service, or
at work now, describing in their own words what they observed. VA gives
weight to specific, first-hand observations; it gives none to opinions
about diagnosis or to generic praise.

Send the witness this template and the form (21-10210, fillable PDF at
va.gov/find-forms). Each witness signs their own; the form has a
certification block. One to two pages is ideal.

Suggested witnesses:
1. **Mother (Kathy L. Bouren)** — listed as nearest relative on the DD214;
   saw him before enlistment and immediately after discharge in July 2004.
2. **A friend from Monroe, MI who knew him at 17–18 and again after 2004.**
3. **A paramedic colleague or supervisor from the last ~5 years** who has
   seen the day-to-day effects at work.
4. (If reachable) **anyone from Troop A, 3/7 CAV in 2004** who saw what
   was happening in the unit — even a short statement is valuable.
