## Notes for the owner (delete before filing)

- Do not diagnose yourself; describe symptoms and let the clinician name
  the condition. The DBQ and nexus letter carry the diagnosis.
- Do not minimize. Paramedics are trained to under-report their own
  problems; VA rates what is on paper.
- Do not embellish. One contradiction with the OMPF costs more than ten
  strong sentences gain. Read the separation packet BEFORE finalizing
  section 3 so dates and events match.
- If there was a specific traumatic event or assault, stop and tell the
  VSO — that changes the form (21-0781) and the framing (PTSD / MST).
