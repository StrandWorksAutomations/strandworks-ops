Tech Scout — 2026-09-07 (delta sweep, 16:30 PDT)

Second run today; the 07:10 report stands. Re-swept AR/glasses, the IFA floor, model releases, 3DGS and world models for the 9 hours since. Nothing shipped after 07:10, and two items the morning sweep missed:

1. ELO Sentinel XR — $599.99, pre-order open, ships October, won Best of IFA 2026. Dual Sony 1080p OLED 120Hz, 52° FoV, 80g, 18h, and a built-in 0 to -5.0D myopia dial. But wired USB-C only, no camera, no mic, no SDK — a wearable monitor, not AR. Does NOT move the 3rdrider blocker. It is the fifth sub-$800 glasses product to ship this week, and it confirms the week's pattern: every vendor hits the price by deleting exactly one of the four factors, and they each delete a different one.

2. Pico Space Pro cleared FCC — filed as TWO devices (headset + separate 'main unit', model B3110), confirming a tethered compute/battery puck. So the Q4 slip is a software decision, not hardware readiness.

Genie 3 API still Ultra-only. World Labs Atlas still waitlist. No parked ideas unblocked; 3rdrider and haptic-mirror-d4rt both WAIT, unchanged. Meta Connect Sep 23-24 is the next real test. IFA closes tomorrow.
