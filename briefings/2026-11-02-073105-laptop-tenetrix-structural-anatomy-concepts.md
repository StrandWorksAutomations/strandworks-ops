# Structural anatomy curriculum concepts

**Tenetrix Insight · Strandworks LLC · Owner review draft · 2 November 2026**

A curriculum for **health-professions students learning anatomy for the first time**. Start with a regional map of whole bones and relationships, then extend it through the upper limb. Schools and organizations are an intended use to evaluate with educators; adoption and effectiveness have not been established.

## The same teaching structure throughout

**Orient → Explain → Predict → Guided practice → Independent transfer → Recap**

Each lesson establishes the person’s side and viewpoint, explains a small set of relationships, asks for a prediction, offers supported practice, then collects a fresh attempt without answer cues. The recap preserves both first answers and corrections. Vocabulary, control labels and feedback follow the same conventions while duration follows the objectives.

## First course: Upper-limb skeleton

**Shoulder girdle → Humerus → Radius and ulna → Carpal groups → Metacarpals → Digits and integrated review.** Only the shoulder-girdle lesson has authored pilot content; the remaining lessons and later courses are proposals.

The first lesson asks the learner to locate and name the primary whole bones, reconstruct the two taught bone relationships, and identify the same anatomy after a change of side or view. A standalone beginner orientation is included in the plan; a separate Foundations purchase is not a proposed prerequisite.

## Audio that walks through the anatomy

The current local demonstration pairs a fixed draft teaching script with complete model cues: camera view, framing, exact structure, highlighting and labels. It has **11 prerecorded parts across six stages**, about **130 seconds of narration**, plus learner-paced activities. That is narration duration, not total lesson time.

The learner can pause, replay, seek, change speed and explore. Moving the model pauses guidance; returning restores the expected scene before the learner chooses to resume. The dashboard packet includes the full transcript and cue sheet. The interactive model and audio player remain in the local review build; they are not embedded in these dashboard pages.

## What the activities measure

Two guided tasks precede ten independent tasks. Naming is collected before later prompts give away the names. Localization, side, relationships and changed-view responses remain distinct. First attempts, hints and corrections stay separate; written explanations require educator review. Reaching the end of the audio does not establish mastery.

These are whole-bone structural lessons using static geometry, without a movement or practical-competence claim.
