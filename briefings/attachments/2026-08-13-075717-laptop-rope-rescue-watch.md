- **Level:** Combined Ops+Tech (I/II). Covers NFPA 1006 Ch. 5 Operations **and** Technician
  skills in one enrollment. **No prior Operations certification required.**
- **Dates:** Mon 2026-10-12 → Fri 2026-10-16. 5 days, 40 contact hours.
- **Location:** Edmond, OK. Travel: Berea KY ≈880 mi / ~13 h drive — practically a **fly-in**

- **Cost:** $1,475 early (registered + paid 45+ days out — 2026-10-12 is 60 days out, so the
  early price applies today) / $1,525 inside 45 days. Lodging and meals **not** included.
- **Accreditation:** NFPA 1006 Ch. 5 content; students receive a **CMC School Certificate of
  Completion**. No Pro Board or IFSAC seal is stated on CMC's page — `accreditation: NFPA 1006
  content, no third-party seal stated`.
- **Prerequisites / gear:** none stated on the class page. Gear provision not stated.
- **Seats:** **Open.** Verified against CMC's own Arlo booking platform: event 1177 returns
  `IsFull: false` with a live register link, while every other RRT I/II date in the window
  returns `IsFull: true` and a waiting-list link. Exact seat count is not published; class is
  capped at 18, and CMC states your spot is not guaranteed until they confirm enrollment
  (they contact registrants ~45 days out to confirm the class runs).
- **Register:** https://cmcpro.arlo.co/register?sgid=5c9289356b6f4726be8fdd10781f0a44

> This is the single best fit in the window: one enrollment, both levels, no Ops prerequisite,
> and it is the **only** non-sold-out CMC RRT I/II date anywhere in the country right now.
