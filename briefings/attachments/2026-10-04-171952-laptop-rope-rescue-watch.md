# The find of this run — Ohio Fire Academy

Not a hit. It is three days of calendar away from having been the best hit of all six runs, and it
needs to be on the Jan/Feb 2027 checklist.

**Ohio Fire Academy — "Hybrid Training: Rope Rescue Technician, NFPA 1006, 2021 edition"**

The title says Technician. The catalog description does not: it covers *"Chapter 5.1 – 5.3: Job
Performance Requirements (JPRs) for Rope Rescue Awareness, Operations, **and** Technician"* and is
explicitly built *"to provide fire personnel with little to no rope rescue skills"* with the
knowledge, skills and abilities of all three levels. That is a combined Awareness+Ops+Tech
enrollment with no Operations prerequisite — the single best-shaped course on this list.

- **Fee: $75.** Not a typo, and not an in-state-only figure as printed in the 2026 catalog.
- **IFSAC certification** on successful completion. The OFA holds 57 IFSAC-accredited
  certifications and claims top-5% international ranking among fire academies.
- **Format:** five online instructor-led classes, then **seven days on campus** at the Academy
  (includes the weekend), plus off-campus sites. Catalog prints 32 hours; the Acadis record for
  the 2027 sessions says 58 h 0 m.
- **2026 deliveries, both now past:** Mar 23–May 1 (online) + May 2–8 on campus; Aug 17–Sep 25
  (online) + Sep 26–Oct 2 on campus. The second one's campus phase ended **two days before this

# What finally loaded this run

Three surfaces that have been dark for multiple runs are now readable, and one general technique
came out of it.

**Acadis portals accept `?PageSize=500`.** Every state academy running Acadis renders its full
course list server-side at that parameter, with **per-session seat counts and an Open/Waitlist
word** in the row. The default page is alphabetical and stops around "D", which is why Mississippi
looked rope-free on the first pass. This is now the cheapest high-signal read in the whole
portfolio and it works on Mississippi and Ohio today. Louisiana already uses Acadis
(still `closed:agency-only`); North Carolina's portal exists but returns zero public events.

**Alabama Fire College is resolved, not blocked.** The 09-20 and 09-27 notes said the calendar was
JS-only and needed Playwright. It is a WordPress site running The Events Calendar, and
`/wp-json/tribe/events/v1/events?per_page=100&start_date=…&end_date=…` returns the whole calendar
in one call. Fifteen events between Oct 4 and Dec 10; none is a rope course. Two runs of "needs
Playwright" were wrong — the answer was a REST endpoint. (Their Nov 11 Alabama Association of
Rescue Squads conference at Orange Beach is unprobed and may carry a rope track.)

**Wisconsin's REACT course page came back.** The seed `ViewCourse.aspx?courseid=82c375bc-…` that
404'd on 09-27 returns HTTP 200 and 81 KB today, with "Available Classes: No classes found."
restored as a clean negative. It also surfaced the gate nobody had seen: WI-TF1 priority plus
mandatory vetting before registration approval. Worth having chased — but the conclusion flipped
from "unreadable" to "readable and closed to him."
