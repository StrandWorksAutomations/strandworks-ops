**Headline shifts of the week:**
1. **FDA opens the first US regulatory framework for generative AI medical devices (Aug 18).** [Discussion paper docket FDA-2026-N-7874](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/digital-health-center-excellence/considerations-regulation-generative-ai-enabled-medical-devices-discussion-paper-and-request) — two-axis risk framework (autonomy of function × severity of incorrect-output consequence), **premarket evaluation modeled on how clinicians are credentialed**, 26 numbered questions, comments due Oct 19. Companion T3000 (865 FP4 TFLOPS, half the size/power) + T2000 (400 FP4 TFLOPS, 16 GB) still Q1 2027.
3. **Google Jetpack XR SDK core libraries reach beta (Aug 21).** [Android Developers blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/08/jetpack-xr-sdk-core-libraries-beta.html) — Jetpack SceneCore, ARCore for Jetpack XR, and XR Runtime graduated from developer preview to beta. Unreal and Godot official support arrives with this update.
4. **OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Sol pricing 20–33% (Aug 21) — first cut since July launch.** Sol dropped $5/$30 → $4/$20 per 1M tokens on a ~3-month promo. This lands in the exact window when Gemini 3.7 Flash's half-price promo ($0.75/$3.75) already restructured the frontier-cost curve — read as OpenAI signaling that Sol needs cost defense now that Gemini 3.7 Flash is beating it on coding + agent benchmarks at 12–20% of the price.
