#### ⚠️ Anthropic text watermarking — shipped (missed by the 08-14 run)

- **Aug 11:** Anthropic announced it will watermark text generated by its models. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/anthropic-says-it-will-watermark-text-generated-by-its-ai-models/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/12/anthropic-claude-watermarks-ai-detection)
- **Aug 14:** Anthropic published the technical explainer, ["How Claude's text watermarking works."](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-text-watermark)
- Mechanism: a variant of **DeepMind's SynthID-Text** (peer-reviewed, *Nature* 2024) — statistical word-choice patterns. **No hidden characters, no extra tokens, no identifying information, no material impact on quality/cost/speed.**
- Scope: **all Claude surfaces** — API, Claude Code, Claude Cowork, and AWS / Google Cloud / Microsoft Foundry deployments. Separately, files Claude emits in `.png`/`.jpg`/`.svg` carry a **C2PA** signed provenance credential.
- **Why it was published:** the **EU AI Act's Article 50 marking obligation took effect 2026-08-02.** This is the compliance surface the last four reports have been tracking abstractly, now made concrete by a vendor. See Project Impact.
- ⚠️ Important limit, stated by Anthropic: the mark proves **processing, not authorship.** [Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16266773-how-claude-marks-ai-generated-content)

## Project Impact

### MedSim-Game (flagship)

**1. The Anthropic watermark is the first item that touches a real MedSim compliance surface, not a hypothetical one.**
Four reports have carried "EU AI Act Art. 50 — live, transparency-only, no action." A vendor has now shipped against it. Two concrete consequences:
- **Generated clinical/teaching text carries a statistical mark.** For the per-coupling explanations behind the clinical-review gate, this is *helpful* — provenance on AI-drafted teaching content is an asset in front of a reviewer, not a liability.
- **The stated limit matters more than the feature:** the mark proves **processing, not authorship**. It does not certify clinical correctness and cannot substitute for the human review gate. Nothing about it relaxes the standing requirement that clinical content is human-reviewed.
- **No action required.** Logged as: the transparency obligation now has a reference implementation.

**2. Qwen3.8-27B is the most interesting cheap-tier candidate to appear in this scout in months — with a caveat.**
- Apache 2.0 + 27B dense + **native image/video** + 262k→1M context is an unusual combination. Genuinely self-hostable, genuinely multimodal, genuinely permissive.
- Against the 08-14 note on Gemini 3.7 Flash: 3.7 Flash is cheaper to *start* and has a **2027-01-01 price doubling** baked in. Qwen3.8-27B has **no price cliff** because there is no vendor meter — the cost is infrastructure. For anything long-lived and high-volume (NPC chatter, scenario-text drafting), the Apache-2.0 option is the one that doesn't reprice in January.
- **Unchanged: clinical-decision arbitration stays on Opus 5.** A permissive license and a vision encoder buy neither clinical correctness nor liability cover. This is a workhorse-tier note only.
- **Do not act on the 2.4T.** Wrong hardware class, and its custom license has revenue/MAU triggers.

**3. Grok 4.6 — noted, no action.** Cheapest frontier-index model at $2/$6, but proprietary and priced above the Flash tier. It doesn't displace either the Opus-5-for-arbitration or the cheap-tier call. Logged so the next run doesn't rediscover it a third time.

**4. GS medical vertical is now explicitly on the radar.** The Radiance Fields issue names **medical applications** as an emerging 3DGS vertical alongside robotics. Worth watching for MedSim scene capture, though nothing actionable shipped.
