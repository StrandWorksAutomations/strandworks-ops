## ⭐ 1. The biggest finding is not a job — it's that the UK degree wall is not real

`vendors.txt` has carried this since 2026-07-25:

> *"UK postings carry a STRUCTURED `Required Education` field (BA/BS/MA/MS) with NO substitution
> clause — this hard-gates the entire Lane 11/12 KY-local surface against an AS."*

**That is wrong.** Every UK posting links to UK HR's published equivalency policy, and the policy
reads, verbatim:

> **"Bachelors degree = associates degree + 2 years of experience in the same field"**
> **"Associates degree = 2 years of experience in the same field"**
> "Masters degree = Bachelors degree + 2 years of experience in the same field"
> https://hr.uky.edu/employment/working-uk/equivalencies

You hold an **AS + 17 years**. Against UK's own table, an AS + 2 years satisfies a required BA. The
`Required Education: BA` field on posting 640561 is **not a wall** — it's a field UK itself tells you
how to substitute against, and posting 640561 links to that page directly from the posting body.

**What this re-opens:** the entire UK HealthCare and UK-campus surface — Lanes 11 (clinical research
coordinator) and 12 (emergency preparedness / EHS / occupational health) — at the closest large
employer to Berea. It has been treated as closed for six days on a bad note. Criteria and vendors
files corrected this run.

**What it does NOT fix:** the G-C domain-experience gate still applies to 640561. The posting wants
**5 yrs related experience**, and "related" means emergency management, not EMS. Two prior UK
Emergency Management recommendations were rejected on exactly this. The degree objection is gone;
the domain objection is not. Your honest case is ICS/EOC experience, after-action reporting, and the
OSCE/austere-ops history — argue it or don't, but it is now a judgment call rather than a hard block.
Also worth knowing before you spend the effort: **the posting requires a polygraph examination** as
part of UKPD's hiring process.
