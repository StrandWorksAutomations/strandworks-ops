## 5. ⭐ The best Lane 1 gate language yet found — and ZOLL posted it in Canada

**ZOLL R19873, Clinical Implementation Specialist I**, posted 2026-08-10. Requirements, verbatim:

> **"RN, Paramedic, or RT with 5+ years of experience required"**
> **"Bachelor's Degree BA or BS preferred"**

That is the exact shape criteria.md has been hunting since Lane 1 was written: paramedic named in the
**required** credential list (not "preferred background"), degree demoted to *preferred*, and a
years-bar he clears three times over. The duties are the Lane 1 archetype end to end — post-sale
implementation, go-live support, adult-education program design, clinical liaison to nurses and
physicians, resuscitation-protocol QI, conference speaking. Bonus heuristics fire on clinical
software product, multi-hospital scope, implementation/training title, and clinical-educator title.
On gate language alone this is a 5.

**It is posted at Mississauga, Ontario with `CA MB Remote` as the second location.** Non-US
postings are a force-skip disqualifier, so it dies at `geo:non-us` regardless of fit.

**But the JD body contains a residue line ZOLL forgot to strip:**

> *"Job Type: Remote — The candidate can be based in **MA/NH/ME**"*

That is a US territory, in a Canadian req, in ZOLL's own template. I searched the entire ZOLL board
for a US twin — `Clinical Implementation Specialist`, `Implementation Specialist`, and `Paramedic` —
and there is none open today. The template proves the US version exists in ZOLL's rotation; it just
isn't posted right now.

**Action: this is a watch item, not an application.** The concrete step is a saved alert on
`Clinical Implementation Specialist` at the ZOLL Workday CXS endpoint, checked every run rather than
only when `Clinical` or `Kentucky` happens to surface it. It also justifies re-reading ZOLL's US
`Clinical Specialist` reqs for the same paramedic-named language — they were previously skipped on
title, not on gate.
