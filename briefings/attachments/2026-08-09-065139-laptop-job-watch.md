## 5. ⭐ Loyal Source is scannable, and the lane is closed negative.

Carried as "still needs browser tier" on 08-07 and 08-08. **No browser was needed — the search page
was simply the wrong door.**

`jm-ajax/get_listings/` returns `found:false`; `wp-json/wp/v2/job_listing` is 404. But the site runs

```
https://www.loyalsource.com/job_listing-sitemap.xml   +   .../job_listing-sitemap2.xml
→ 1,338 job URLs, one curl, no auth
```
