## 5. ⭐ Wanderly is not a scraping problem. It is an account problem. Question closed.

The 08-07 report called Wanderly *"the single highest-value unfinished item"* and attributed the empty
result set to a missing session token requiring the browser tier. **That is settled now, and no browser
would have fixed it.**

| Probe | Result |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/v2/jobs/search` — 5 param combinations incl. no filters at all | `{"success":true,"data":[],"message":"Search Pay Packages"}` every time |
| `POST /api/v2/jobs/search` | rejected |
| `GET /api/v2/professions`, `/api/v2/specialties`, `/api/v2/jobs` | HTTP 404 |
| **`GET /api/v1/jobs/search`** | **HTTP 302 → `https://www.wanderly.us/nurse/login`** |

The v1 route states the policy the v2 route hides: **pay packages are visible to registered users
only.** Wanderly is a rate-comparison marketplace; the rates are the product. An unauthenticated
caller gets `data:[]` by design, forever.

**Action, and it is a five-minute one: register a Wanderly account at
https://www.wanderly.us/nurse/login.** Once a session exists this becomes the single best-yielding
URL in Lane 14 — pre-filtered to Paramedic, multi-agency, rates displayed. Until then it yields
nothing, and no further engineering on it is worth doing.
